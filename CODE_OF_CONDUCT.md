# Code of conduct

Atlas is built by students and for students. Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else it lives, is expected to follow this.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are new to open source, and some are still at school.
- Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep it honest: Atlas teaches and organises. Do not ask for, build or share anything meant to cheat on assessed work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's age, background, identity, ability or experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting copyrighted study material, exam papers or other people's personal data.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers privately through the email on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow this code.
